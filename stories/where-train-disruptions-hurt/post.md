Which six kilometres of Dutch railway hurt the most passengers?

Counting disruptions only tells you where the railway breaks. So I weighted every disruption of 2025 by the number of people who use that stretch of track, using Rijden de Treinen's open disruption data and the passenger counts per track section that NS publishes in its annual report dashboard.

The answer barely changes from year to year: the line between Schiphol Airport and Amsterdam, much of it in the tunnel under the airport. It had a disruption for 817 hours in 2025, about 34 days, on track that carries some 125,000 NS passengers a working day. It ranks first in six of the last seven years. The exception is 2020.

The Randstad as a whole takes about 79% of the journeys caught by disruptions, on under a third of the track.

What this doesn't show:
– exact headcounts: it assumes travel is spread evenly over the day
– partial and full disruptions apart: both count as disrupted hours

The map is interactive: hover any section for its numbers and causes.

Data: Rijden de Treinen (CC BY 4.0), NS annual report dashboard. Map, data and code: https://jawdat-rana.github.io/unexpected-signals/stories/where-train-disruptions-hurt/
